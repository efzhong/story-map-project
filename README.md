Philly Streetlight Improvement Project Story Map

An internal draft for a Storymap project on the Philly Streetlight Improvement Project. Prepared ahead of a conversation with the City of Philadelphia's
GIS team, to walk through the Street Poles dataset and a few specific
issues found while working with it.  This is an interactive scrollytelling map exploring Philadelphia's citywide LED
streetlight conversion (PSIP) and a gap in the open data meant to track its progress.

Highlights:
- What PSIP is, why it exists, and how it's funded
- Program highlights of safety, sustainability, jobs, and cost-neutrality
- A University of Pennsylvania study finding a 21% reduction in nighttime
  gun crime in areas where the new lights were installed
- What the Streets Department actually owns versus other utilities
- A close look at four Philadelphia neighborhoods with the highest
  concentration of firearm-related violent crime
    - What the City's streetlight records do and don't confirm about their conversion status

public infrastructure data to push for streetlight repairs and upgrades.

Built with Leaflet, Mapbox, JavaScript, using a scrollytelling template from PointsUnknown.
